Outdoor Adventure Club

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Name: Outdoor Adventure Club

Reasoning: This name directly reflects the club's mission to organize local hiking, camping, and outdoor exploration activities. It is memorable, descriptive, and clearly communicates the purpose to prospective members.

Site Purpose

The site serves as a central hub for outdoor enthusiasts in the community. It provides information on upcoming group hikes, safety guidelines, gear recommendations, trail reviews, and a registration form for new members to join the club.

Scenarios

  • Question 1: How do I register for an upcoming weekend group hike and what difficulty level is it?
  • Question 2: What essential gear and safety equipment should I bring for a beginner-level trail excursion?

Color Palette

The selected colors reflect a natural, outdoorsy environment:

  • Primary Color (#1b4332 - Forest Green): Used for headers, top navigation, and major emphasis areas.
  • Secondary Color (#2d6a4f - Sage Green): Used for section headings, border highlights, and secondary buttons.
  • Accent Color (#d8f3dc - Mint Green): Used for callout box backgrounds and hover states.
  • Neutral Text/Background (#2b2b2b & #f8f9fa): Light background with dark slate text for clear contrast and readability.

Typography

Headings Font: Montserrat (Sans-serif) – Bold, modern, and clean for clear section visual hierarchy.

Body Font: Open Sans (Sans-serif) – Highly legible across various screen sizes for reading longer paragraphs and lists.
